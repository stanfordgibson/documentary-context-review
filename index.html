Settings and help

About Context Cut

Context Cut helps documentary editors assess whether a selection may change the meaning of an interviewee’s words. Compare an excerpt with its source, inspect evidence and suggested remedies, then record your own editorial decisions.

Current demonstration: one entirely fictional Naledi interview, three predefined excerpts and predefined review observations. No live AI analysis occurs and no interview text is sent to a model. Source/edit imports and in-app passage selection belong to the next product phase.

Limits: the tool does not independently verify facts, footage, source authenticity, corrections or context elsewhere in a documentary. Review completion is not truthfulness certification.

Your data: decisions stay in this browser, do not sync and are not encrypted by this app. They can be lost if browser storage is cleared. Local and hosted addresses have separate browser records. Reports contain saved decisions only; downloaded copies remain wherever you save them.

Help

Quick start
  1. Choose one of the three excerpts.
  2. Read its words in the annotated source and inspect the relevant question and context.
  3. Review the potential meaning change, source evidence and suggested remedy.
  4. For each finding, record and save a decision with a rationale. Separately save your editorial decision about the excerpt.
  5. Preview the saved report, export text or choose Print / Save as PDF.
Understanding the transcript
Excerpt
The proposed words selected for an edit. Each demo excerpt is a separate selection, not part of an assembled final film.
Source transcript
The original interview text against which that selection is reviewed.
Speaker turn and source ID
A turn is one continuous question or answer by a speaker. T04, for example, is the stable identifier for Naledi’s answer about free screenings.
Timestamps
Locate the full answer or question, not individual highlighted words.
KEPT / OMITTED
Included in or excluded from the active excerpt only. Omitted words may appear elsewhere in a documentary; the prototype does not check that. Green means kept, not approved.
Understanding the review
Finding
A concern requiring human consideration, not a verdict.
Material meaning change
A consequential change in what an audience may understand, such as losing a condition or the question identifying what an answer means. Ordinary shortening is not itself a concern.
Source evidence
Authentic quoted passages and references supporting the concern. Follow a reference to inspect the source.
Suggested remedy
Source words to restore or a clarification to consider. A suggestion does not apply an edit.
No concern flagged
No concern was identified by this review. It is not approval or proof that the edit preserves meaning.
Insufficient context / Review unavailable
The review cannot provide a current assessment. Neither state clears the excerpt; inspect the source yourself.
Review method and version
Identify the predefined demonstration observations used. The version is not your number of reviews or the documentary’s edit version.
Making decisions
Needs attention
No saved assessment has closed the finding.
Addressed
Record how you dealt with the concern, for example by restoring a qualification or supplying context elsewhere.
Dismissed
Explain why you believe the concern does not apply or warrant a change. The finding, evidence and suggested remedy remain in the report.
Keep / Revise / Leave out
Your separate editorial decision about the excerpt. Keep does not automatically dismiss findings; Revise does not change the transcript.

Addressed and Dismissed record your assessment and rationale. Neither applies an edit or verifies a change to the documentary. Explanations about context elsewhere are recorded, not verified.

Example: the excerpt omits the rent-free-hall condition. Choose Addressed and explain how you restored or supplied that context; separately choose Revise for the excerpt. The tool records your explanation—it does not apply or verify the edit.

Moving around

Click or use the keyboard to activate kept passages and open their meaning review. Review links beside other example passages switch to that excerpt. Evidence links and Back to source passage return to source text. The excerpt selector stays available while scrolling.

Focus on this passage collapses distant turns while retaining the question and nearby context. Show full transcript restores them. Jump to meaning review moves you directly to the analysis panel.

Saving and reporting

Each finding and excerpt decision needs a rationale and its own Save action. Check the saved confirmation. Unsaved text is excluded from the report.

The executive summary counts material findings separately from excerpts. Needs attention covers findings without a current saved decision; Not decided covers excerpts without a current saved editorial decision. Outdated, malformed or unavailable reviews cannot count as completed.

Incomplete reports can be exported and show open concerns. The review is complete only when all excerpts have saved decisions and every material finding has an explicit saved decision. Completion does not certify truthfulness.

Preview saved report shows the current saved record. Export internal report downloads text. Print / Save as PDF opens your browser’s print dialog; select its PDF destination to save a PDF.

Accessibility

Choose Labels only for a colour-free source display. KEPT and OMITTED are explicit text; colour is not the only status indicator. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and the visible focus outline to track your position. Native selection controls follow your browser’s keyboard conventions.

Help topics expand with Enter or Space. Escape or Close closes this panel and returns focus to the settings wheel. Opening Help preserves unsaved work.

Troubleshooting
Saved decisions are missing
Use the same browser profile and address where you saved them. Clearing site data can remove records; local records do not transfer to the hosted demo.
Storage blocked or save failed
A “Not saved” message means the app could not confirm persistence. Check browser storage permissions before relying on a saved decision.
Outdated review
The source, selection or review identity no longer matches. Reassess it; old decisions are not treated as current.
Counts differ from your selections
Reports use saved decisions only. Save each finding and excerpt decision, then generate a fresh preview.
Latest update is missing
Reload the published page. Save your work before reloading: unsaved text will be lost. Avoid clearing site data to refresh, because that also removes saved decisions.
